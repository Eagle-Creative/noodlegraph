# How we treat each other here

This is a place to build something together. A few things keep it that way.

- **Be kind.** Assume the other person means well and knows something you do not.
- **Talk about the work.** Say what a change or an idea does and where it falls short, not what
  you think of the person who brought it.
- **Make room.** Questions from people new to the project are welcome. So are answers that
  take the time to explain.
- **No harassment.** No insults, no threats, no unwelcome personal attention, no publishing of
  someone's private details. Not here and not in private messages about the project.

If something is wrong, write to Eagle Creative (https://eagle-creative.com). It is read by a
person, kept confidential, and acted on: from a quiet word to removing someone from the project.
